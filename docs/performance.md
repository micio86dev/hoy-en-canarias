# Performance and quality measurements

All numbers below were measured on this machine against the Docker stack (`web` on port 3300, production build), with Lighthouse 12 and its default throttling. They are not guarantees for a real deployment.

## Lighthouse (final build)

| Page | Preset | Performance | Accessibility | Best practices | SEO | LCP | CLS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | desktop | 99 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 0.8 s | 0.03 |
| Home | mobile | 83 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 3.5 s | 0.02 |
| Search | desktop | 99 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 0.8 s | 0.01 |
| Search | mobile | 80 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 3.8 s | 0.04 |
| Event detail | desktop | 100 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 0.6 s | 0 |
| Event detail | mobile | 82 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 3.6 s | 0 |
| Map | desktop | 98 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 1.1 s | 0 |
| Map | mobile | 72 | 100 | 96 | 100 | 5.4 s | 0 |

The brief asks for 90+ on representative pages where the environment allows it. Desktop meets it on every page. Mobile does not. Diagnostics point at JavaScript: about 150 KB of the client bundle is more than a third unused, and the render waits for the entry stylesheet and chunks under simulated 4G. The map's mobile LCP element is an OpenStreetMap tile, and this sandbox reaches that server poorly, so its number says more about the network than the app.

Next steps, in order: split the largest client chunk, defer non-critical widgets on the home page, and measure again on a real device.

Before reserving the map's height during loading, its CLS was 0.13 on desktop and 0.24 on mobile; after the fix both are 0.

## Accessibility

- axe (WCAG 2.2 AA tags) runs on home, search, islands and map, on desktop and mobile: no violations.
- Colour contrast is tested for every text/background token pair.
